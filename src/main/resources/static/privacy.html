Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

StockSim is a stock trading simulator built as an academic project. This page explains what information it keeps about you and why. It is short because StockSim collects very little.

What we store

  • Account details: your full name, username and email address, entered when you register.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way BCrypt hash. Nobody, including the project maintainer, can read your actual password.
  • Account dates, such as when your account was created.
  • Simulated activity, such as your virtual balance and, once trading is available, the virtual trades you place.

What we don't collect

StockSim never asks for bank details, card numbers, PAN, Aadhaar or any other financial or government identifier. No real money moves through the app at any point.

Cookies

StockSim uses two cookies, both required for the site to work:

  • JSESSIONID keeps you signed in during a visit.
  • XSRF-TOKEN protects your account from cross-site request forgery.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent to the server. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. Your account information is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. To have your account and its data removed, contact the project maintainer and it will be deleted from the database.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
